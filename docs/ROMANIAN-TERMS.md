# Romanian terminology — our store against competitors (17 Nov 2026)

Sampled: metamob.ro (a Romanian blade manufacturer, `/ro/` pages), panza-banzic.ro, werkland.ro, plus search
snippets of simonds.ro, eMAG, Hornbach and Pentagon. Counts are word counts on the pages fetched, not search volume.
Our side: every Romanian string in `messages/` (1 006 strings). Not checked against a dictionary or a native speaker.

| Term | Competitors | Our RO store | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **banzic** (everyday word for a band saw) | panza-banzic.ro 28×, werkland 47×, simonds "Pânză banzic pentru lemn", eMAG "panglică/banzic"; metamob 2× | 0× | Use it in titles, H1, meta descriptions, category and product names ("Pânze panglică banzic") |
| fierăstrău / ferăstrău | mostly **fierăstrău** (panza-banzic 41 of 53, werkland 13 of 19, Hornbach); a dictionary lists "herăstrău" as a rare variant of fierăstrău | **ferăstrău** everywhere (23×) | Switch to fierăstrău / fierăstraie |
| set of the teeth | **ceapraz / ceaprăzuit** — metamob 47× (service "Ceaprăzuire", "dinți ceaprăzuiți"), werkland 6× | **avoiere / avoiat** (10×) — on no competitor page | Switch to ceaprăzuire / ceaprăzuit (confirm with a native speaker) |
| tooth pitch | "pas" + **TPI**: werkland 30× TPI ("Grosime și pas", "×6 TPI"); panza-banzic "Tip dantură 10/14 TPI"; metamob "Pasul [dinți/inch]", "dantură variabilă" | "pas" 33×, "TPI" 1× | Keep "pas"; write TPI beside every value (10/14 TPI); "dantură" for the tooth pattern |
| band blade | "Pânze panglică" (metamob 114×) | "Pânze de panglică", "Pânze de ferăstrău panglică" | "Pânze panglică" |
| categories | by use: "pentru debitat bușteni", "tâmplărești", "pentru debitat metale", "pentru tăiat textile, burete și carton" | by material and by machine: "Ferăstraie orizontale/verticale", "Ferăstrău hobby" | Name the blade, not the machine: "Pânze panglică orizontale (gater)"; "hobby" → "tâmplărie / hobby" |
| decimals | comma in text ("0,9") on werkland and eMAG; some dots in titles | dot in product names ("32×1.1") | Comma in the Romanian store ("32×1,1") |

## Applied 17 Nov 2026 (owner confirmed "ceapraz" is right)
"banzic" in the Romanian titles, descriptions, hero and menu; fierăstrău/fierăstraie everywhere (also the public
category addresses, e.g. `/category/fierastraie-panglica-orizontale`); ceaprăzuire / ceaprăzuit instead of
avoiere / avoiat; "Pânze panglică" instead of "Pânze de panglică"; decimal comma in Romanian product names and sizes;
